# v0.0.263

Release quality ranks before the reported-peer threshold. A seeded 1080p release no longer loses to 480p or 720p solely because it reports two seeders instead of three. Zero-seeder releases remain excluded; 720p and older SD releases remain available as fallbacks. Source, trusted-uploader and peer preferences still apply. Recognition preference cannot lower the resolution across that threshold.

Completed-series Manual Pick puts advertised packs first and enables **Season packs only** by default. Filtering and ordering happen before the bounded candidate cap and pagination. Turn the checkbox off to reveal individual episodes for fallback. Releases with unspecified coverage remain visible, including unlabelled BD packs. Pack labels are advertised hints; file inspection separately reports how many requested episodes the plan covers. BD/1080p+ and audio controls remain available. Airing and film searches retain their existing behavior.

Auto-Pick with a saved active-watch group preference uses the normal acquisition worker and its durable per-episode two-hour deadline. Mode changes, restarts and unknown release dates do not erase the wait. Historical catch-up stays immediately eligible, and late preferred releases cannot duplicate active targets. Fresh previews retain their preference when committed. At deadline expiry, an exact saved fallback is freshly revalidated and cannot remain postponed solely by partial search results.
